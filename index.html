Meet Your Coach

Experience, Coaching & Leadership

Hey there, I'm Krista.

I began my real estate career in St. Louis, Missouri, in 1999, earning Rookie of the Year honors before moving into leadership and talent development. As Director of Career Development and Training, I built recruiting and performance systems to attract, develop, and retain talent across multiple brokerages.

I joined Keller Williams in 2011 and became part of its leadership team in 2012. My roles included Team Leader, Productivity Coach, and Director of Production, and I also invested in Keller Williams market centers.

In 2019, I joined MAPS Coaching, expanding my work with real estate producers and coaches across the United States, Portugal, and Canada.

My approach is direct and practical: strategic clarity, thoughtful questions, accountability, and execution that supports long-term growth and leadership with integrity.
